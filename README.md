One funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner. The program writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I counted. You opened me 14 times today and took nothing. I am not a television.

Twelve original candidates were hand-scored against the judge's brief (fridge's voice, a real joke with a sting, smug about its sensors, a small domestic grudge, fresh concrete detail); the winner scored highest and, among the tied top four, is the shortest. A second pass revised the winner ("ate nothing" → "took nothing": nobody eats standing at the fridge) and re-scored it still the best.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
